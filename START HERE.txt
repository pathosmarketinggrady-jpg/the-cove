THE COVE SOCIAL CLUB
Website files
===============================================================================

EXTRACT THIS FOLDER FIRST.

Do not open the website from inside the zip. Right-click the zip, choose
"Extract All", pick a location, then open the extracted folder. If you open
index.html from inside the zip it will look broken, because Windows does not
give the page access to its stylesheet or images until the files are extracted.


TO VIEW THE SITE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser. No internet connection is
needed. Nothing needs installing.


THE PAGES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
index.html        Home. Hero, the weekly calendar, The Wednesday Pour,
                  the Fall Cohort offer, the spaces.
events.html       The full events calendar.
venue.html        The four spaces and private hire.
membership.html   The Fall Membership Cohort offer and member privileges.
inquire.html      Contact details and an enquiry form.
