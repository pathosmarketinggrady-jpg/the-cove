The venue

Jacksonville's premier creative event venue

A thoughtfully curated space for an intimate gathering or a dynamic pop-up, with warm lighting, rich textures and intentional design.

ONESpace Showroom

A gallery-style showroom for exhibitions, launches, pop-ups and seated dinners, under three ring chandeliers and a full wall of gilt mirrors.

The Lounge

Exposed brick, leather chesterfields and a fireplace. The room the club is built around, and the one most members end up in.

The Lab Café

The artisan café at the front of the club, open to members and guests through the day, with the street and the neighbourhood on the other side of the glass.

The Event Floor

A flexible floor for receptions, networking, performances and private hire, dressed to whatever the night calls for.
